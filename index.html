Scan photos

Any number of cards per photo: one card, a grid, or a whole deck spread out so every title is visible. The tool reads card titles, not the pile.
Fastest on a phone: lay out 12-18 cards in rows with every title visible and take one photo per batch. Or take photos in your camera app and pick them all here at once.
Tips for accurate scans
  • Lay cards flat in rows. Overlap them so only the title strip of each shows, or spread them with a little gap.
  • A stacked pile can't be read: only the top card's title is visible.
  • Light from two sides or from above at a slant, and tilt the phone slightly so the reflection is not directly under the lens.
  • Shoot as sharp and close as you can. If a photo of many cards finds few, retry with Small-text mode or split it into 2-3 photos.
  • Copies: the tool counts how many times it sees a title. If you scan the same card in two photos, it counts twice. Fix quantities on the review list.

Live scan (camera)

Hold cards up one at a time. Browsers only allow the live camera on https or localhost pages. If this page opened as file:// or content://, run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the folder with this file (Windows: py -m http.server 8000) and open http://localhost:8000/mtg-toolkit.html.
